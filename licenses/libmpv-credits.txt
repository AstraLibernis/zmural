libmpv-2.dll: credits
=====================

zmural's Windows release plays video and pictures with libmpv-2.dll, a
build of mpv (LGPL) by the mpv-winbuild project:

  https://github.com/zhongfly/mpv-winbuild
  release 2026-09-26-a1bf4b6559, mpv-dev-lgpl-x86_64-20260926-git-a1bf4b6559.7z
  built with https://github.com/shinchiro/mpv-winbuild-cmake

That one file holds the work of everyone below. They are listed from the
build recipe (mpv and FFmpeg, and what they are built with, minus what the
LGPL build leaves out). A few are headers or helpers used while building.
Each project's licence text is in its repository, at the link given.

Thank you all.

and AviSynth.

replaced with any compatible libmpv build. See THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md.
